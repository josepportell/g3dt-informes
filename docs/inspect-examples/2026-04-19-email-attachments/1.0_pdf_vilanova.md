## Hypothesis — which stage to blame

If I had to bet on the single stage most responsible for the downstream miss, I'd point at **stage 0.45 (ConceptScout probe gate)**. Here's why:

- The text-extractable gate excluded `1.0.pdf` from the vision probe. Had the probe run, it would have almost certainly classified it as `architect_plan` (the same probe on Rubí's `IMG-20251104-WA0015.jpg` returned exactly that for a similar floor plan, with 0.8 confidence and `document_type="architect_plan"`).
- With a probe cache saying `architect_plan`, stage 0.46 Layer 0 would have trusted it directly, skipping the conservative Layer 2 vision call entirely. Result: promote to `architect_plan` role → Vilanova gets its missing role.
- That cascades: stage 1 `planol` extractor runs on the file, produces signals at priority 20 (beats Groq's 45), architect_name becomes MATCH, `num_floors` + `superficie_parcela_m2` + `superficie_construida_m2` move from NE to extracted.

Stage 0.46 (the deep-folder vision call) is a **secondary cause** — the model made a bad judgement call (architect_plan → situation_plan). Better prompt or 2-of-3 voting there would also fix this. But stage 0.45's filter came first in the chain and is a cleaner, more systematic fix.

Stage 0.4 (Groq) is the **producer of the wrong answer**, not the root cause. Fixing Groq's architect extraction would improve this case but leave the structural gap: Vilanova would still be missing its `architect_plan` role.
