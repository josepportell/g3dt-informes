- **Qwen3 32B as default** — best quality/price balance
- **No multi-model + judge** — over-engineering; Eva reviews in 30s anyway
- **Developer plan pending** — eliminates 60s rate limit waits
- **json_object not json_schema** — Groq models don't support json_schema format
